# F5 results: shrinking the NMS radius is a net cost, dose-dependently, at every budget

Date: 2026-09-04. Run: `f5_nms_radius_ablation.py`, 2,203 s, 14 ROIs × 5 seeds × 3 radii ×
6 z × 2 axes = 5,040 arms, 45,360 rows. Pre-registered in
[`2026-09-04-f5-preregistration.md`](2026-09-04-f5-preregistration.md) (revision 3, converged
after two rounds of adversarial review, committed at `c2be222` **before** the sweep finished).

**All 3,741 checks passed.** Read `results/f5_nms_radius_ablation_verification.csv` before any
table below.

---

## 1. The headline

Holding border padding ON in every arm and moving only the NMS radius, **shrinking it makes
retrieval worse** — at every budget from K = 10 to K = 10,000, monotonically in the dose, and
significantly at the ROI-cluster level.

**Arbiter** (pre-registered §8): mean-of-5 **paired** Δ(recall@250) per ROI, z = 1.0,
`chromatin_od`, 14 clusters, exact 2¹⁴ sign-flip + cluster bootstrap, Holm across 2.

| dose | mean paired Δ | 95 % cluster-bootstrap CI | sign-flip p | Holm p | cell Wilcoxon (anti-conservative) |
|---|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `r5.9` (23.3–26.1 px) | **−0.0148** | [−0.0206, −0.0091] | 0.00098 | 0.00195 | < 1e-12 |
| `r5.0` (19.7–22.1 px) | **−0.0223** | [−0.0316, −0.0135] | 0.00098 | 0.00195 | < 1e-12 |

Both CIs exclude zero. The sign-flip p is at its attainable floor: 11 of the 14 ROIs have a
strictly negative mean Δ and 3 are exactly 0.0000, so the effective enumeration is 2/2¹¹.

**Not one cell in 70 has `r5.0` beating the control at K = 250** — 71.4 % are strictly worse and
the remainder tie.

### The registered falsifier, and which way it fell

With P4 withdrawn before the run (§2 below), the surviving mechanism test was **monotonicity
across the dose**: if the effect is the same-object secondary peak v2 traced, `r5.9` must sit
between `r7.5` and `r5.0`. It does, in **95.7 %** of the 70 cells. This is an ordered effect of
suppression, not noise that happened to land negative.

### It is an active intervention, not an inert one

`topk_churn` against the control is **0.249** (`r5.9`) and **0.321** (`r5.0`) — a quarter to a
third of the top-250 list changes identity, with 5,435–5,682 ground-truth claim reassignments.
This is the column F4 §6 built an entire pre-flight to obtain, and it is what makes the null
interpretable: the radius genuinely moves the list, and the list it moves to is worse.

## 2. What was withdrawn before the run, and why it matters to reading this

Revision 1 justified the two doses as a **mechanism decomposition** — 5.9 µm to stop merging
distinct annotations, 5.0 µm to additionally stop the same-object secondary peak. Measured over
all 14 ROIs, that is not available:

| radius | mitotic↔mitotic pairs inside | any↔any pairs inside |
|---|---:|---:|
| 7.5 µm (control) | **1** (245.tiff) | **7** (1 on 245, 6 on 403) |
| 5.9 µm | 0 | 0 |
| 5.0 µm | 0 | 0 |

**Zero** pairs lie between the two doses, so they are identical with respect to un-merging ground
truth, and across ~1,325 mitotic annotations exactly one pair is close enough for the *default*
radius to merge at all. The ladder was re-registered as a one-mechanism dose–response and P4
withdrawn — before any result existed, which is the only time a withdrawal is worth anything.

This also **retracts an overstatement revision 1 made about F4**. F4's claim ("shrinking cannot
recover a merged pair of ground-truth mitoses") is about the 5.0 µm radius, and 6.594 µm > 5.0 µm
holds on the 14-ROI set. F4 was right; revision 1 was not.

## 3. The three false-positive questions, kept apart

The request was "does our candidate list have fewer FPs overall". That is three questions, and
only the second and third are independent of §1.

**(1) At fixed K — the same question as §1.** `FP@K = K − TP@K`, so precision@K is an affine
restatement of recall@K. Reported, labelled as such, not offered as corroboration. (The
`budget_delivered == budget` guard fired on 3 of 3,780 cells, none at the headline.)

**(2) Over the full list — worse, and necessarily so.**

| domain | `r7.5` | `r5.9` | `r5.0` |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| pooled ratio to control | 1.000 | **1.235** | **1.373** |
| canine lymphosarcoma | 16,879 | 24,872 | 31,760 |
| canine soft tissue sarcoma | 12,441 | 13,808 | 14,396 |

A shrink suppresses less, so more candidates survive; this was known before the run and is
reported in one line rather than dressed up. The domain spread is large — lymphosarcoma nearly
doubles, soft-tissue sarcoma grows 16 %.

**(3) FPs paid to reach a recall target — worse at every target.**

| target | `r7.5` | `r5.9` | `r5.0` | coverage-guarded? |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 0.80 | 632 | 725 | 783 | no |
| 0.90 | 1,786 | 2,034 | 2,326 | no |
| 0.95 | 3,544 | 4,100 | 4,559 | no |
| 0.99 | 5,753 | 7,352 | 8,157 | **yes — see §5** |
| 1.00 | 5,871 | 8,243 | 9,102 | **yes — see §5** |

The two guarded rows are the ones the pre-registration refuses to let carry a claim. The unguarded
rows say the same thing anyway.

**Duplicate detections on already-claimed objects**, reported with the denominator that makes them
comparable across arms of different length: `dup_fp_frac` rises **0.0027 → 0.0043 → 0.0047**. This
is precisely the defect `invariants.check_nms_radius` exists to prevent, reappearing
dose-dependently — the invariant was right.

Reading depth moves the same way (`read_50` 128 → 142 → 156; `read_95` 3,634 → 4,189 → 4,648),
which reproduces the direction of the 2-ROI sweep in
[`2026-09-01-tm-variant-sweep.md`](2026-09-01-tm-variant-sweep.md) §5f on a balanced sample.
`read_50` is a diagnostic here, never an arbiter (D4 §5).

## 4. What the shrink *does* buy, stated so it is not lost

The tail improves, and the improvement is real:

| | mean full-list recall | worst-of-70 |
|---|---:|---:|
| `r7.5` | 0.99745 | 0.96629 |
| `r5.9` / `r5.0` | 0.99982 | 0.99231 |

And the **win/loss ledger is clean**: **24 gained, 0 lost**, at both doses, across 70 cells and
6,555 annotation-instances, with gains concentrated in 13 of the 70 cells. Greedy NMS's
non-monotonicity in the radius — real, and the reason nesting was deliberately not asserted —
never actually bit on this data. So the shrink does not shuffle; it adds.

**But it is a bad trade at the operating point.** ~24 annotation-instances recovered, for 23–37 %
more candidates, a quarter-to-a-third of the top-250 list churned, and −0.015 to −0.022 recall at
K = 250. And §5 shows part of the tail gain is not evidence at all.

## 5. Coverage — why the tail gain is not the headline it looks like

| z | `r7.5` | `r5.9` | `r5.0` |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.5 | 0.894 | 0.928 | 0.935 |
| **1.0** | **0.837** | **0.871** | **0.879** |
| 2.0 | 0.675 | 0.703 | 0.711 |
| 3.0 | 0.396 | 0.406 | 0.410 |

A shrink keeps more peaks → raises coverage → mechanically raises full-list recall. Coverage rises
with the dose at every z, so the §4 tail gain arrives partly by geometry. This is why
`full_list_recall` was pre-registered as never-a-headline, and why the same guard was extended to
`read_99` / `read_100` / `FP_to_1.0` — otherwise the saturated regime is exactly where a shrink
would have looked like it won.

## 6. The dose grid was mis-centred on two domains — the check that caught it

The 23–29 px same-object suppression band carried the entire justification for where 5.9 and 5.0
sit, and it came from **one ROI and one seed** (v2 cells 9–11). Re-measured on all 14 ROIs × 5
seeds, at the control radius:

| domain | gap p05 | median | p95 | n |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| canine cutaneous mast cell tumor | 11.9 | 22.7 | 28.4 | 26.8 |
| canine lymphosarcoma | 13.9 | 22.9 | 28.7 | 28.8 |
| human neuroendocrine tumor | 20.4 | 25.9 | 29.9 | 6.0 |
| human melanoma | 17.3 | 24.6 | 26.3 | 2.9 |
| human breast cancer | 18.4 | 22.1 | 26.4 | 2.9 |
| **canine soft tissue sarcoma** | **16.9** | **17.8** | **18.7** | 1.1 |
| **canine lung cancer** | **10.9** | **12.1** | **15.6** | 0.4 |
| pooled | 16.2 | 22.5 | 26.4 | — |

v2's 23–29 px was near the **top** of the true distribution, not typical of it. On canine lung
cancer (10.9–15.6 px) and canine soft tissue sarcoma (16.9–18.7 px) the whole band sits *below*
even `r5.0`'s 19.7–22.1 px, so on those two domains no dose in this ladder addresses the mechanism
at all — every arm suppresses the entire band.

This does not rescue the negative result: those are also two of the domains where the shrink
costs least, and the effect is negative in 11 of 14 ROIs regardless. But it means **a
correctly-centred ladder for those domains was never tested**, and any future attempt should size
the radius per domain from this table rather than from 301.tiff.

## 7. What this means for the pipeline

**v2's recall improvement came from the border padding, not the radius.** The two were changed
together and reported together; separated, padding is the part that helps and the radius shrink is
a net cost that padding's gain masked. The defensible configuration is **padding ON, NMS radius
back at the 7.5 µm default** — which also restores `invariants.check_nms_radius` rather than
overriding it.

The named mechanism v2 traced on 301.tiff is real (24 clean gains, 0 losses, dose-monotone), but
it is worth about 24 annotation-instances in 6,555 and costs 23–37 % more candidates. At the
operating point the product actually uses, that is the wrong trade.

**Verification that makes the above trustworthy rather than merely computed:**

* `r5.0` at seed 0 reproduced `results/tm_ccoeff_threshold_axis_sweep_v2.csv` to
  `max_abs_recall_diff = 1.1e-16` and `max_abs_ndet_diff = 0` over 336 cells × 7 ROIs on **both**
  axes — the whole pipeline validated against an independent earlier implementation.
* `check_min_separation` on every arm at its own radius (3,570 checks); `check_nms_radius` on the
  control as a positive control; the one-match-many-z shortcut at z = 1.0 **and z = 3.0** for all
  three radii (84 checks); `od` hoist equivalence; `valid.all()`, `od_nan == 0` and
  `n_gt_within_seed_hole == 0` asserted per cell.

## 8. Limitations

* **One template configuration** (single 51 px, `TM_CCOEFF`, `hematoxylin_od`). The same-object
  gap is a property of the correlation surface, hence of the template, so §6's table does not
  transfer to a tightened template — that is F4's question.
* **n = 14 clusters** bounds resolution: the smallest attainable two-sided sign-flip p is 2/2¹⁴,
  and with 3 exact zeros the realised floor here was 2/2¹¹.
* **recall@250 is ceiling-adjacent** on the four ROIs carrying 17–19 evaluation mitoses
  (201, 233, 013, 529); three of the four contribute the exact-zero Δs, diluting the pooled effect
  toward zero. The effect is therefore, if anything, understated.
* **The cost estimate was wrong.** §10 predicted ~9–12 min; the run took **37 min**. The estimate
  came from a single `246.tiff` probe and under-counted the per-arm evaluation work (three arms ×
  six z × two axes of `bucket_detections`, plus cached `coverage_fraction`). Budget ~2.5 min per
  ROI for a re-run of this shape.
