Reading this chart. Each lane spans precision 0–1. The light dot is precision at the 7.5 µm default; the dark dot is the same ROI re-run with NMS suppressing at 5.0 µm. A visible connector means the radius change reordered the top-K list enough to change who's in it — most lanes show none. Pooled across all 14 ROIs, the ablation moves precision by at most 3.2%, and not consistently up or down. 245.tiff — this dataset's one documented merged-mitotic-pair case — is the exception: shrinking the radius keeps both peaks separate and recovers a true positive at K = 20 and K = 30.
